## Correction

The earlier privacy warning could be read as prohibiting *all* student-identifying information in *all* AI tools. NSW Education specifically distinguishes its approved, department-operated NSWEduChat from unapproved consumer tools: some personal identifying information and student work can be entered into NSWEduChat, while sensitive or confidential material should still be withheld. The p-l4 activity now reflects this distinction. The p-l6 activity now identifies NSW public school Years 5–12 access, schoolwork-only use, teacher/school assessment rules, and disclosure/citation expectations. Updated the NSW source link to the current department page and added eSafety's chatbot risk advisory. Reviewed date updated for the supplemental activity set.
