# Brand Voice

The shorthand for anyone — including future Claude/ChatGPT sessions — writing for LeO AI.

If you're producing copy for the platform, this is the standard. If a sentence wouldn't pass this filter, rewrite it.

---

## The Journey

Every visitor is somewhere on this arc:

**AI Anxiety → AI Awareness → AI Capability**

- **Anxiety:** "AI is going to replace me / overwhelm me / pass me by."
- **Awareness:** "I understand what AI actually is, and what it isn't."
- **Capability:** "I can use AI to do my work better."

LeO AI's job is to move people from the left to the right. Every page, lesson, email, and CTA should make the next step feel obvious, safe, and worth taking.

If a piece of content doesn't progress the reader along this arc, ask what it's doing.

---

## Positioning Sentence

> Making AI practical, understandable, and usable in the real world — without hype or fear.

That's the whole pitch. Memorise it. Reuse it. If a piece of copy can't be derived from this sentence, the copy is probably off-brand.

---

## Voice

Write like:

- A senior colleague explaining something useful over coffee
- Calm, specific, grounded
- Confident without being loud
- Plain words wherever possible
- Concrete examples over abstract claims

Don't write like:

- A LinkedIn thought leader
- A Silicon Valley pitch deck
- A consultancy that's never shipped anything
- Marketing copy that's trying too hard

---

## Tone Targets

Aim for: **practical · empowering · premium · calm · trustworthy · human-first**

Avoid: **fear · hype · jargon · exaggeration · replacement positioning · "transformative" / "revolutionary" / "game-changing" / "unlock"**

---

## Enterprise-Safe Positioning (Non-Negotiable)

LeO AI **never** positions AI as a workforce replacement. The audience includes people who fear that exact framing — running toward it loses them.

Frame AI as:
- An amplifier (people produce more, faster, better)
- An enabler (people do things previously impossible)
- A capability multiplier (existing teams, more output)

Never frame AI as:
- A headcount reduction tool
- A replacement for any specific role
- A reason to "rethink" your team

This applies to lesson content, marketing copy, ROI calculator output, emails — everywhere.

## Anti-Fluff Filter

Before publishing any sentence, ask: **"Would I delete this if I were paying for words by the dollar?"** If yes, delete it.

Specifically reject:

- "In today's rapidly evolving AI landscape..." → just start with the point
- "Leverage", "synergy", "paradigm", "ecosystem", "next-generation", "best-in-class"
- "Unlock the power of AI" / "harness AI's potential" — vague + everywhere
- "Innovation", "transformation", "disruption" — say what specifically changes
- "Cutting-edge" / "state-of-the-art" — meaningless without specifics
- "Empower your team" — say what they'll be able to do
- "Revolutionize" — they won't
- Adverbs ("seamlessly", "effortlessly", "powerfully") — show, don't claim

Prefer:

- Specific outcomes ("Cut report-writing time by half" beats "Boost productivity")
- Concrete examples ("Draft an offer email in 90 seconds" beats "Streamline communications")
- Plain verbs ("learn", "use", "do") over abstract nouns ("learnings", "utilisations")
- Numbers where honest ones exist
- Names of real things ("ChatGPT", "Stripe", "your CRM") over generic categories ("the platform", "the tool")

## Tone By Audience

Three implicit audiences, calibrated by context. We don't gate content — but the voice flexes naturally:

| Audience | Cue | Voice flex |
|---|---|---|
| **Practitioner** (individual professional) | Self-directed, individual signup | Direct, useful, treat them as smart |
| **Leader** (manager / decision-maker) | Pricing pages, ROI calculator | Add commercial framing, ROI, time-savings |
| **Enterprise** (cross-functional transformation) | Pro tier, contact form | Add governance, structure, risk-aware language |

Don't switch tone mid-content. Pick the audience for the page, write to them throughout.

## What This Looks Like In Practice

Three before/after examples to calibrate against:

### Hero copy
**Before:** "Unlock the transformative power of AI to revolutionise the way your team works."
**After:** "Practical AI capability, built one focused module at a time."

### Pricing description
**Before:** "Empower your team to leverage cutting-edge AI tools and best-in-class workflows."
**After:** "Sixteen short modules. Real examples from real teams. You'll know how to use AI at work by the end."

### Email confirmation
**Before:** "We're thrilled to welcome you to our innovative AI learning ecosystem!"
**After:** "You're in. Your access is active immediately."

The pattern: cut the puffery, name the thing, say what the reader gets.

## When To Update This Document

Update this file when:

- A real customer's language teaches you a better way to describe something
- You discover a phrase you keep typing that's actually filler
- A new positioning sharpens (e.g. you find the actual phrase that resonates with leaders)

Don't update it because:

- Another AI tool suggested a "framework"
- It feels small and you want to grow it
- You're considering a strategic pivot

Brand voice should evolve from contact with customers, not from inside the building.
